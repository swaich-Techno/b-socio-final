B Socio Vercel Environment Variables

Add these in Vercel:
Project Settings > Environment Variables

MONGODB_URI=mongodb+srv://USERNAME:PASSWORD@CLUSTER.mongodb.net/b-socio?retryWrites=true&w=majority
JWT_SECRET=make_a_long_private_random_secret_here
ADMIN_EMAIL=your_owner_email@example.com
NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://bsocio.in
CONTACT_RECEIVING_EMAIL=connect@bsocio.in

Important:
- Do not upload .env or .env.local to GitHub.
- Contact page already works through WhatsApp and email links.
- MongoDB is only needed for optional lead saving and future dashboard work.
